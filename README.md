# How I Signed In to Lightning Faucet with Wallet of Satoshi

Lightning Faucet offers several ways to earn small amounts of bitcoin over the Lightning Network. I wanted to see how difficult it was to get started without creating another password or depositing money. This walkthrough covers the part I tested personally: signing in with Wallet of Satoshi using a Lightning QR code and reviewing the available earning options.

## What you need

- A phone with Wallet of Satoshi installed
- A second screen or browser displaying Lightning Faucet
- No deposit for the sign-in process

Never share your wallet recovery phrase or private information with a website. The login flow I used only required scanning a QR code and approving the login inside the wallet.

## Step 1: Open the earning page

I started at [Lightning Faucet](https://lightningfaucet.com/earn/microjobs/) and opened the microjobs section. The page lists several possible rewards, including social posts, written reviews, blog posts, and one-time rewards for following the project on public social profiles.

The reward amounts vary. The site says that short posts receive smaller rewards, while useful tutorials and detailed reviews can receive more. Submissions are reviewed manually, so copying generic promotional text is unlikely to help.

## Step 2: Choose Lightning Wallet login

I selected **Log in** and kept the **Lightning Wallet** option active. The browser displayed a QR code and a “Waiting for wallet” message. Wallet of Satoshi was listed as a compatible wallet.

## Step 3: Scan the QR code

In Wallet of Satoshi, I opened the scanner and scanned the QR code shown in the browser. The wallet asked me to approve the login. This was an authentication request, not a payment request: I checked the prompt before confirming it.

After approval, the browser refreshed automatically. The login button was replaced by an account menu and a balance of zero satoshis, confirming that the account had been created or connected successfully.

One practical tip: keep the browser tab open until the wallet approval has returned to the site. If the tab is closed or refreshed too early, the QR session can expire and you may need to scan a new code.

## Step 4: Review the available earning methods

Once signed in, I could view microjobs and a daily check-in. The daily check-in starts at one satoshi, but the page clearly states that this reward goes to a bonus balance with a play-through condition. Because I was looking for earnings without gambling, I did not use that option.

The content tasks were more relevant. At the time of my test, Lightning Faucet listed:

- Short social posts with rewards based partly on audience size
- Written reviews on supported public platforms
- Blog tutorials and detailed reviews
- One-time rewards for following the project from a public profile

## My honest first impression

The wallet-based login was quick once I kept the QR page open. I liked that I did not have to create and store another password. The site also shows reward ranges and rejection rules before submission, which makes the expectations relatively clear.

I have not tested deposits, casino games, or withdrawals, so this article does not make claims about those features. I will update my assessment only after a real task is reviewed and paid. Anyone trying the platform should read the terms, avoid depositing money they cannot afford to lose, and verify every wallet prompt before approving it.

For the same starting point, visit the [Lightning Faucet microjobs page](https://lightningfaucet.com/earn/microjobs/).
